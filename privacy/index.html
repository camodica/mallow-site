Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026.

Mallow does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts with us, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What Mallow talks to

Mallow connects only to plex.tv, to sign you in and find your servers, and to the Plex Media Servers your account can reach, to browse and play your media. What those services do with your data is covered by Plex's privacy policy and by whoever runs the server.

What stays on your device

Your Plex sign-in token is kept in your device's Keychain. Settings, recent searches, cached artwork and library data, and diagnostic logs are kept on your device and never leave it unless you choose to share a diagnostics file yourself.

Crash reports

If you have opted in to sharing analytics with Apple, Apple may share crash reports with the developer. These don't identify you.

Children

Mallow is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
