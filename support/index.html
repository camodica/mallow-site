Support

Need help with Mallow, or found a bug? Email SUPPORT_EMAIL. In Mallow, Settings › Diagnostics can export a log file to attach (it never includes your Plex token).

What do I need?

A Plex account and access to a Plex Media Server, your own or one shared with you.

Mallow can't find my server

Check the server is running and signed in to the same Plex account. Away from home, turn on Remote Access in the server's settings. On iPhone, allow Local Network access for Mallow in Settings › Privacy & Security.

A video won't play or stutters

Try a lower quality in Mallow's player menu, or check the server's transcoder settings. Exporting diagnostics and emailing them helps us find the cause.

Is Mallow made by Plex?

No. Mallow is an independent app and isn't affiliated with Plex, Inc.
